Hero Headline Framework

What Is a Hero Headline?

A hero headline is the first promise your product makes to a potential customer.

Its purpose isn't to describe the product—it's to convince the right person to keep reading.

---

## Principle #1 — Lead With the Outcome

People don't buy software.

They buy the result the software creates.

### ❌ Weak

AI-powered automation.

### ✅ Better

Save 5 hours of repetitive work every week.

---

## Why It Works

Strong hero headlines focus on the customer's desired outcome rather than the product's features.

Instead of describing what the software is, communicate what the customer will achieve.
